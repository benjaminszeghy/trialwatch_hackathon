SCORING GUIDE

One priority, two inputs

Review priority blends the highest associated law concern and the opportunity for TrialWatch to help, with equal weight. Both inputs remain visible in each case.

50% law concern + 50% TrialWatch fit

Law concern is rescaled from /12 to /100 before combining. Scores are provisional review indices, not probabilities or findings of misuse. These weights and thresholds are demo assumptions, not official TrialWatch intake policy.

Your feedback changes the queue

Answer “Law misuse?” and “TrialWatch fit?” in one card and save once. They remain two independent human labels for later adjudication and training. You may answer either question; unanswered cases contribute no human vote.

Misuse Yes raises the feedback-adjusted score of your selected law; No lowers it. The original rubric has the weight of six reviews: (6 × original + 12 × Yes) / (6 + Yes + No). The original rubric is always preserved.

Fit Yes adds 20 to the automatic fit score, with a floor of 45. Fit No sets automatic fit to 0 and caps non-urgent combined priority at 39, behind active candidates. Same-law checks from other cases adjust automatic fit by up to 15 points. Checked support needs replace the automatic fit score. A fit No de-prioritizes the case without erasing these facts or an urgent need. This is a transparent feedback reranker, not a newly trained model.

Evidence and urgent needs

The initial fit index checks reporting connections, specific support-gap passages, an opening to act and source freshness. Missing facts are unverified, not proof of no need. Generic keyword mentions cannot establish that a journalist lacks counsel or interpretation. An old court order cannot establish today’s custody or hearing status.

You can refine checked counsel, interpretation, defence access, timing and practical openings. A verified immediate threat, deadline within 7 days, or custody without counsel overrides the blended score. No does not suppress a verified urgent need or close a review. Unknown scores remain ranges; source changes require recheck. Closure requires a reviewer’s reason.

COLLECTION CONTEXT

Coverage & freshness

What is covered

A source check is not a new court filing

Publication can lag court activity. Act and section metadata may be older than order metadata; cases without matching law metadata cannot enter this collection yet. An order dated after July 2024 does not by itself establish that the offence is under BNS.

How to use these documents

Short procedural orders may not state the charges. A metadata link, text reference or AI-assisted review note still needs independent legal verification. New records have no misuse or legitimacy labels. Separate proceedings and multiple orders can belong to the same underlying prosecution.
