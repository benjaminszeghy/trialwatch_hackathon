General of Prisons, Maharashtra rejecting his Application for furlough leave.

Ms.Mhatre, learned APP for the State. Peruse the record and the  Affidavit of
Mr.Pramod Wagh, Superintendent of Taloja Central Prison, Navi Mumb ai,
dated 11th October, 2025.

on parole leave on 10 th November, 2005, he over stayed for a period of 352
