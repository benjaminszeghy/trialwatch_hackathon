# Claude collaboration — BNS acquisition

Kai explicitly requested that Codex and Claude work together on BNS scraping and case selection. Codex sent the task to the existing Claude “Google Docs link” conversation, then supplied the collection counts and requested a bounded final review. Claude found official court sources; Codex independently downloaded six reference PDFs and checked supporting passages. Three newly found references record target BNS charges. All remain pending human/legal validation.

Adopted refinements:

- Separate actual charges, procedural provisions, precedent references, constitutional challenges and unknowns.
- Preserve FIR family and cross-court/cross-state connections. CNR counts are retrieval units, not independent FIR counts.
- Extract the FIR narrative with Act-bound sections, dates, police station and evidence; do not rely on a bare number or on the order year.
- Label document type, source of an assertion, alleged conduct, expression nexus and dispute context separately.
- Preserve unresolved subsection and offence-date information.

Two suggestions in the initial review were corrected and confirmed with Claude:

> 两点都同意，也纠正我前面的说法：Ruchika Mitra 不应直接称“难负例”，应按文本分别标表达、纠纷类型和身份。30 个 CNR 经独立人工核对前只算待核对标注，不算金标准。

Accordingly, private/non-journalist cases receive no automatic non-abuse label, and AI annotations are not promoted to gold. No teammate, shared document or project repository was messaged or edited through Claude. The collaboration concerned acquisition and design only.

Unverified leads were not promoted to official-source cases: Kunal Kamra, Mohammed Zubair, Sajid Chaudhary and Foundation for Independent Journalism. A failed official-source request supplies no conclusion about the case or the server’s geographic access policy.
